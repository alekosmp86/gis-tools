# Routine jobs: "Carga de ejes de calles", splits, merges and removals (EJS-1 to EJS-16) - PARTLY BUILT (slice A: merges and plain removals; slice C: splits with automatic point assignment; slice D: the per-point review of the review points, EJS-6, and the choices of EJS-16; the per-point result list of EJS-12 is NOT BUILT)

Refines EJ-13, EJ-19 and EJ-21 of `ROUTINE_JOBS_EJES_CALLES_SKELETON_SPEC.md`. Slice 1 (`..._LOAD_SLICE1_SPEC.md`) applied none of this. Slice A applied accepted merges and plain removals. Slice C applies accepted splits whose points all resolve automatically (EJS-3, 5, 7, 8, 14, 15, 16 for the server and payload). Slice D is the per-point review of the review points (EJS-6, and the choices of EJS-16 in the UI): a split with review points is accepted only when each of them has a choice of the user.

Status markers: BUILT (slice A or C, as stated), PARTIAL (what is built and what is not), NOT BUILT.

## Facts (DEV ONLY numbers; nothing below may be assumed on preprod or prod)

- `carto.access_point` has no geometry. Point geometry: `carto.address.geometry` via `carto.address.id = carto.access_point.id` (SRID 32721). `bdj_carto` is never a source of points.
- `access_point.id_road_section` stores the section `reftramo` (not `id`); no FK; about 68k points match neither reftramo nor id and are never touched.
- Splits: 469, of which 192 hold points; 737 points, all associated by construction to the old section's reftramo. 648 have a clear nearest piece (second nearest at least 5 m farther); 36 are under 1 m apart, 89 under 5 m; 8 are within 1 m of the line.
- 281 points are more than 25 m from every piece (median 31.4 m, max 49.7 m, none over 100 m). All 281 are equally far from the OLD section (within 1 m of the same distance) and all have `access_point.id_circulation_way` equal to the section `idcalle`. Conclusion: the distance is lot offset from the street centreline, not a defect; nearest piece stays valid and 25 m is not an exclusion threshold.

## Criteria

- EJS-1 (PARTIAL: merges, plain removals and splits per row, with the per-point review of EJS-6; the per-point result list of EJS-12 NOT BUILT): Every split, merge and removal is applied only through its per-row "Aceptar" (EJ-13), never automatically and never by "Aceptar todos" alone without the review. A split with a review or no-geometry point is acceptable in the UI only when every one of them has a choice of the user (slice D, EJS-6); until then it shows "Requiere revisión".
- EJS-2 (BUILT, slice C): The `ejes-address` read role (default `carto.address`) provides `id` and `geometry`. The layout resolution verifies that the geometry SRID is exactly 32721 (`EJES_SRID`). A wrong SRID (or an empty geometry) is information in a dry run; in a real run it blocks only when `accepted.splits` is non-empty, because only the split points read the address geometry. The analysis treats such points as having no geometry, so no SRID is mixed and no suggestion is made. Point geometry is read only from it, joined by `access_point.id`, never from `bdj_carto`. Allowed staging inputs stay: `bdj_carto.ine_localidades_pg` and the ejes staging table. Access points are never deleted, orphans included; the only write on `access_point` is `id_road_section`.
- EJS-3 (BUILT, slice C; piece rule revised per EJ-10 of the skeleton): which new sections are pieces or merge members is the one predicate `isPieceSql`: covers >= `COVERAGE_RATIO` of the old section, or covers > `SPLIT_PIECE_MIN_COVER_M` (10 m) and >= `PIECE_OWN_FRACTION` (50%) of its own length. Split (one old, many new): each point of the old section (by `reftramo`) goes to the nearest of the new pieces of its split (distance from `address.geometry`, `ST_Distance`, both SRID 32721); ties are broken by the lower `reftramo` (numeric). Only the pieces of that split are candidates. The piece geometry used for distances is the staging geometry of the piece (one per `reftramo`, the same one the split coverage uses). Locality twins (copies of one `reftramo` in several localities) share identical geometry: verified on dev (0 groups with differing geometry). The analysis still refuses (`not_single_copy`) a piece whose copies have different geometries, so a distance can never be read from a geometry the load does not write.
- EJS-4 (BUILT, slice A): Merge (many old, one new): the points of every old section go to the single new section (`id_road_section` = its `reftramo`). No distance rule is needed.
- EJS-5 (BUILT, slice C): Automatic resolution is maximised. `AMBIGUOUS_POINT_GAP_M` = 5 m. A split point resolves automatically to the nearest piece when there is one candidate, or when (distance to second-nearest piece) - (distance to nearest piece) >= `AMBIGUOUS_POINT_GAP_M`. Distance to the line is not a criterion (no maximum distance, see EJS-8). Every other point (gap under the constant, EJS-7, or any other unresolved case) is a review point (EJS-6).
- EJS-6 (BUILT, slice D, revised after the Opus review): Review of the unresolved points of each split, in the splits list ("Revisar puntos" per split with review or no-geometry points). The dry run decides the points and keeps them: the analysis computes the review points (`reviewPointCtes` in `domain/splitPointSql.ts`: each point with its candidate pieces, distance in metres to one decimal, nearest first) as `reviewPoints` of the analysis, and the job of the dry run keeps them server side (`api/ejesReviewStore.ts`, keyed by `jobId`; `withReviewRetention` strips them from the job result, so status polls never carry them; they are dropped with the job). A split whose points would pass `REVIEW_POINTS_STORE_LIMIT` (20000) is not stored whole: its row is flagged `incompleteReview` and it is not acceptable. Each split row carries `heldReason`, the reason the load holds the split whatever the user chooses (`splitGroupsSql` in `domain/splitHoldSql.ts`, the one builder the analysis and the load plan both read: no reftramo, repeated old reftramo, points by id, shared piece, copies not exactly one); a held split is not acceptable and the list shows that reason. The read `POST ejes-calles/split-points` takes `jobId`, the five tables it reads by key or small lookup (staging, road section, access point, address, circulation way; `EJES_SPLIT_POINTS_ROLES`) and the page window; it reads the stored points of (jobId, oldRef) and fetches only the door fields, the point geometries and the point's `idcalle` by primary key, the old section (its `idcalle`, by reftramo) and the pieces by reftramo, and the street names by `idcalle` (`circulation_way.id_calle` = `idcalle`, the lowest `circulation_way.id` of each street, as `carto.v_address_build`), in one read-only transaction with the geometry-map timeout. The page answers each point's `pointStreet` (`{idcalle, name}`, or null when the point has no `idcalle`; `name` is null when no way of the street has one) and the page's `oldStreet` (the same shape for the old section, null when the section is not found or has no `idcalle`). The screen shows the point's street and the old section's street, one line "Calle: NAME (N)" when they are the same street and two lines ("Calle del punto", "Calle del tramo anterior") otherwise, with the warning "La calle del punto no coincide con la del tramo anterior" when both are known and differ; the warning is informational and changes no choice. No other street (its section, its distance or its name) is read or shown. No analysis runs in the read. A job that is no longer kept answers 400 "El análisis ya no está disponible. Vuelva a analizar." and the screen shows "El análisis cambió, vuelva a analizar.". If the stored total differs from the summary of the split, the screen warns and disables choosing. The screen (`ui/ejes-calles/splitReview/`): the modal shows "N de M puntos resueltos" and the reason filter; one point at a time with Anterior / Siguiente ("N de M"); a button per candidate to choose it (choosing it again clears it); "Ver en mapa" shows the point, the old section and every piece, each piece in its own colour (`MAP_CANDIDATE_TYPES`, five colours; a sixth and later piece is drawn in the new-section colour), with the chosen and the suggested piece named on the map; "Aceptar sugeridos" sets the suggestion of every ambiguous point that has none chosen yet (never a no-geometry point), capped by `ACCEPTED_CHOICES_LIMIT`. The choices live in the accept state of the wizard (keyed like the acceptance, EJ-24) and are sent with `accepted.splits[].choices` only for accepted splits. The accept of a split is one helper (`toggleSplitAcceptance`) used by the list and by the modal; the choice rules are one hook (`useSplitChoices`). The splits list filters by reason (ambiguous / sin coordenada) and by street (`idcalle`), and shows the progress of each split. Server side (BUILT): a split is applied only when each of its points is resolved (automatically or by a user choice in the accepted payload, EJS-16); otherwise it is held with `review_required` and reported. A split with review points is acceptable in the UI only when it is not held, its points were all stored, and every review point has a choice (`isSplitAcceptable`).
- EJS-7 (BUILT for the rule, slice C; the list is EJS-6): A point with no `carto.address` geometry (or with a geometry of another SRID) is never resolved automatically: it is a review point with reason `no_geometry`, with no distances and no suggestion; only a user choice resolves it.
- EJS-8 (BUILT, slice C): Only points already associated to a section (`access_point.id_road_section` = the old section's `reftramo`) are ever reassigned. Points with another or no association are never touched. A split with a point linked by `road_section.id` is held (`points_by_id`) and its old section is never deleted. Far points (over `FAR_POINT_REPORT_M` = 25 m from every piece) follow the normal rule: the offset is lot offset, not a defect (Facts), so nearest piece applies and 25 m is no threshold; they are only counted (EJS-14).
- EJS-9 (BUILT, slice A): Plain removals keep EJ-21: a section with access points is never deleted; one without is deleted with its address master.
- EJS-10 (BUILT, slices A and C): Order inside the one transaction: insert new sections and masters (the pieces of applied splits included), reassign points (merges and renumbers by `reftramo`; split points one UPDATE per point with its own guard), then delete old sections and masters. No row is deleted before its points are reassigned. A failed count or guard aborts the whole transaction.
- EJS-11 (BUILT, slices A and C): The gzip revert SQL (EJL-4) restores the old `id_road_section` of every moved point (split points by id, grouped by their old `reftramo`), the deleted rows (split old sections included) and deletes the inserted ids (split pieces included).
- EJS-12 (PARTIAL: the split, merge and removal counts and lists are built, and `pointsMoved` counts split points; the per-point result list is NOT BUILT): The result lists every reassigned group and every skipped one with its reason; the per-point list of the applied load (old and new `reftramo`, distance, automatic or user choice) is NOT BUILT. The per-point review of the dry run (EJS-6) is built. Server real-run switch (EJL-16) and topology (EJ-20) apply unchanged.
- EJS-13 (BUILT, slice C): Reproducibility. The load runs on dev, preprod and prod, whose data may differ. All thresholds (`AMBIGUOUS_POINT_GAP_M`, `FAR_POINT_REPORT_M`, `SPLIT_PIECE_MIN_COVER_M`, `COVERAGE_RATIO`, `COVERAGE_TOLERANCE_M`) are named constants; no dev number (469 splits, 737 points, 281 far, etc.) is assumed in code, tests or copy.
- EJS-14 (BUILT, slice C): The analysis (and so the preflight, which shows it before "Cargar ahora") counts per environment the split points by `reftramo`: total, automatic, review (ambiguous), no geometry, and far (informational). Missing geometry does not block; those points are review points (EJS-7). A wrong address SRID blocks a real run only when splits are accepted (EJS-2).
- EJS-15 (BUILT, slice C): The analysis report states these environment-specific counts (`splitPointsTotal`, `splitPointsAuto`, `splitPointsReview`, `splitPointsNoGeometry`, `splitPointsFar`), and each split row shows its own automatic, review, no-geometry and far counts, as measured in the environment being loaded. The accept summary sent back carries the three counts that decide acceptance, so a change in them between the analysis and the load is `ANALYSIS_CHANGED`.
- EJS-16 (BUILT, slices C and D: accepted payload with choices, validation and revert (slice C); the choices are made on the review screen of EJS-6 (slice D)): The user's choice per review point is part of the accepted payload (`accepted.splits[].choices[{pointId, newRef}]`, sent only for accepted splits and only with the user's own choices, never a point resolved automatically; at most `ACCEPTED_CHOICES_LIMIT` choices and `ACCEPTED_KEYS_LIMIT` splits, refused above, never truncated) and of the revert SQL (EJS-11). A choice whose point is not a review point of its split, or whose piece is not one of its split's pieces, or that names an automatic point or a held split, aborts the load before any write with `CHOICE_NOT_APPLICABLE_MESSAGE` ("Se eligió un destino para un punto que no requiere revisión o para una división que no se puede aplicar. Vuelva a analizar."). An invalid choice aborts the load before any write and names up to INVALID_CHOICE_REFS_LIMIT (5) old eftramos (choiceNotApplicableOf, uildInvalidChoiceRefsSql). The split plan reads the same splitGroupsSql hold reasons as the analysis's heldReason, so a choice on a split the load holds is refused, never silently skipped.

## Decisions (orchestrator, slice A)

- Held groups (Q1). A new section that belongs to a split (its pieces) or to a merge (its new `reftramo`) is inserted only when that group is accepted and applied. For every merge and split that is not applied, the new refs of the group are held: not inserted, no section, master or way created for them, and the old sections stay untouched. The group is reported once in the skipped list with its old and new refs and its reason (`not_accepted`, or the refusal reason). A merge whose new `reftramo` is also a split piece is refused with `shared_piece` (slice C; it was `split_pending` in slice A).
- Skipped list (Q2). The result carries the first `DETAIL_LIST_LIMIT` skipped rows, the total (`skippedTotal`) and the exact count per reason (`skippedByReason`), computed over every row, not only the listed ones.
- Unknown keys in `accepted` are a 400 (Q3). Unknown keys inside a split entry or a choice are a 400 too (slice C).

## Decisions (slice C)

- Piece rule (revised). The 2 m cover of a neighbour was too short: on dev a NEW section that only touches the end of a same-street old section overlaps it 4-5 m at a bend (old 1001462 / new 1030829, 4.0 m; old 1001823 / new 1030832, 5.0 m), and such a neighbour made a false `shared_piece` hold both splits. Now `SPLIT_PIECE_MIN_COVER_M` = 10 m, and a piece must also cover `PIECE_OWN_FRACTION` = 50% of its own length (or absorb 95% of the old section). Known limit: a long NEW section that passes over a short old one (under half of its length on it) is no longer a piece; such a case is a plain removal plus NEW sections unless the old section is absorbed. Re-check on dev with the analysis before the next real run.

- Split refusals (held, reported once): `not_accepted`, `no_reftramo`, `repeated_old_ref`, `points_by_id`, `shared_piece` (a piece of another split, or a new `reftramo` of a merge), `not_single_copy` (a piece without exactly one kept copy), `review_required` (a review point without a choice). Checks run in this order and the first one wins.
- Accepted keys the in-transaction analysis does not hold abort the load with `ANALYSIS_CHANGED_MESSAGE` before any write. Choices are validated in the transaction (see the choice decision below).
- The point assignment is built once, in the analysis transaction, by data-modifying CTEs that fill `ej_split_pts`; the load reads that table, so the analysis and the load agree. The plan (`ej_split_plan`), the final assignment (`ej_split_final`, with the raw old `reftramo` of each point) and the delete list are built before the backup is written.
- The split point UPDATE is guarded per row by the old `reftramo` (`accessPointRefMatchSql`), and its count must equal the planned count. Its ordering: after the pieces are inserted and the renumbers moved, before the old sections are deleted.
- Address geometry SRID is deferred from the layout resolution to the preflight (`addressIssues`): information in the dry run; in the real run a blocker only when `accepted.splits` is non-empty.
- A repeated `reftramo` (several current sections): its splits get no point rows in the analysis (`cur_rows` must be 1), so point totals are not double counted. Such splits are refused (`repeated_old_ref`) anyway.
- Choices are checked after the split plan exists: a choice for an automatic point, for a point or piece outside its split, or for a split the plan holds aborts the load with `CHOICE_NOT_APPLICABLE_MESSAGE` before any write (fail closed, never a skipped row).
- The accept summary carries `splitPointsAuto`, `splitPointsReview` and `splitPointsNoGeometry` (the counts that decide what can be accepted).
- Follow-up (not in this slice): the Spanish accept labels ("Aceptar", "Rechazar", "Aceptar todos", "Rechazar todos") are duplicated between the padron review and the ejes review. Move them to ui-kit later; no refactor now.
